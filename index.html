Bolivia Real-Time Inflation & Retail CPI Tracker

Comparing high-frequency daily supermarket-based retail Consumer Price Index (CPI) estimates against official Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE) benchmarks.

Dashboard Highlights

  • National Trends: Real-time daily supermarket indicators compared against the national INE Core-5 and general CPI basket.
  • City-Level Trackers: Custom daily index tracking and inflation calculations for major cities (La Paz, Santa Cruz, and Cochabamba).
  • Category Breakdown: Detailed side-by-side analysis for Alimentos, Bebidas, Bienes diversos, Muebles, and Prendas de vestir.

Methodology & Economic Research

Developed by Thomas Riveros (Cornell University), this project aims to provide daily, high-frequency visibility into price adjustments across Bolivia. Through automated supermarket scrapers and robust statistical rebasing methods, this dashboard helps bridge the gap between monthly retrospective benchmarks and real-time local price conditions.
